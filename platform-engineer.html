Platform Engineer · Senior / Staff

I build paved roads and get every engineer on them.

Platform Engineer was my literal title at CyberGRX and Cloaked, and the pattern was the same both times. Find where shipping hurts, build the paved road, then do the unglamorous adoption work of docs, demos, and pairing until the platform is simply how work gets done.

{% include "partials/hero-ctas.html" %}

Platforms with adoption numbers

A platform nobody uses is a hobby. Mine get used.

3 days
to org-wide adoption

At CyberGRX I redesigned the build and deploy process, presented it, and had every engineer in the org shipping with it within 3 days. Deploys went from quarterly manual events to on demand.

30x
faster deploys at Cloaked

I led the migration from a legacy PaaS to a multi-account AWS EKS platform meeting SOC 2, ISO 27001, and ISO 27701 requirements, with a GitOps CI/CD pipeline that accelerated deployments 30x.

Self-serve
CDK patterns other teams consume

At Inception Health I designed the CDK patterns application teams used to ship HIPAA-compliant microservices themselves, and built mobile CI/CD that turned TestFlight and App Store releases into self-serve deploys from git.

AI-era
platforms for coding agents

At Block Party I designed and built the framework behind the company’s internal AI coding bots. The newest platform customer is the agent, and the paved-road playbook works on them too.

My first 90 days on your platform team

1 · Find where shipping hurts

I sit with the teams doing the work and trace the slowest, most error-prone parts of getting code to production.

2 · Build the paved road

Tools and defaults that make the right way the easy way, treated as a product with the engineers as customers.

3 · Drive adoption personally

Docs, a presentation, and pairing until usage is universal. Platforms earn their keep through adoption, and I treat that as my job.
