How I run a team

The same three moves at every stop, with a receipt behind each one.

1

Make delivery easy

I find the slow, error-prone parts of shipping and rebuild them into tools the whole team actually uses.

Receipt

At Food Service Warehouse the teams on my infrastructure ran the company’s fastest release cycle. At Inception Health I mentored the product and mobile teams on CDK, serverless, and CI/CD until they shipped their own App Store builds without waiting on anyone.

2

Bring visibility to the team’s work

I get real metrics in place so decisions stop being guesses and everyone can see what’s actually working.

Receipt

At Cloaked I built the product-analytics stack the whole company used, so roadmap calls got argued from the same numbers.

3

Ship the small first version, instrumented

I put an embarrassingly small v1 in front of real users with tracking from day one, then let how they use it decide what’s next.

Receipt

Ravi’s v1 shipped instrumented from day one, and its usage data carried the $400K raise.
