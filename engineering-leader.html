How I run a team

Each with the receipt behind it.

1

Make delivery easy

I find the slow, error-prone parts of shipping and rebuild them into tools the whole team actually uses.

Receipt

At Food Service Warehouse the teams on my infrastructure ran the company’s fastest release cycle. At Inception Health I mentored the product and mobile teams on CDK, serverless, and CI/CD until they shipped their own App Store builds without waiting on anyone.

2

Make the work visible

I put real metrics in front of the whole team, so decisions get made from numbers everyone can see.

Receipt

At Cloaked I built the product-analytics stack the whole company used, so roadmap calls got argued from the same numbers.

3

Keep releases small and instrumented

I keep the team shipping in small, tracked increments from the first release, so product decides the next step from real usage.

Receipt
