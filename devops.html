Quarterly deploy events become shipping on demand.

When I joined CyberGRX, deploys were quarterly, manual, and brittle. When I left, teams deployed whenever they wanted. At Inception Health I automated TestFlight and App Store releases through git, so mobile teams shipped their own builds without waiting on anyone. The deploy arc applies to app stores as much as clusters.

My first 90 days on your pipeline

1 · Time the current path

I measure how long merge-to-production actually takes today, including the waiting and the fear, so the improvement has a baseline.

2 · Automate the scary parts

The steps people double-check by hand are the steps that belong in the pipeline. GitOps makes the pipeline itself reviewable.

3 · Publish the metrics

Deploy frequency and lead time on a dashboard everyone sees, so faster shipping is a fact rather than a feeling.
